# KshetraOS — Hub71 Pitch Brief

## One-Line
KshetraOS automates rural loan follow-up for Indian microfinance institutions using WhatsApp and n8n.

## Problem
60 million rural borrowers in India receive loans through MFIs and cooperative banks.
Loan officers manually call each borrower for repayment follow-ups.
Call answer rates in rural areas: 30–40%.
Result: avoidable NPAs, officer burnout, and borrower exclusion from credit systems.

## Solution
Mihar Follow-Up Desk — a no-code n8n automation workflow that:
- Reads borrower data from Google Sheets
- Auto-sends WhatsApp reminders 3 days before due date
- Escalates to field officer if no response in 48 hours
- Logs all interactions back to Google Sheets

## Why Now
- WhatsApp penetration in rural India: 530M+ users
- n8n enables zero-infra deployment
- MFIs actively looking for low-cost digital tools post-COVID

## Vision
KshetraOS becomes the operating layer for rural financial institutions across South Asia and MENA — starting with loan follow-up, expanding to credit scoring, crop advisory, and government scheme disbursement.
